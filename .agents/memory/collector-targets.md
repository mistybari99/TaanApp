---
name: Collector weekly targets
description: Weekly cash handover targets for collectors and their relationship to member payment entry.
---

A collector's weekly target is a cash-custody benchmark for accepted handovers during the current Saturday-Friday period. It is not a member assignment filter or a payment-entry restriction: collectors may still record payments for any cycle member, including payments they receive on behalf of direct Owner relationships.

**Why:** Some members pay the Owner directly, so a collector can be responsible for handing over a fixed weekly amount without needing to collect every member's money.

**How to apply:** Measure progress from accepted collector-originated handovers, show pending and remaining amounts separately, and keep target configuration at the active-cycle level.
