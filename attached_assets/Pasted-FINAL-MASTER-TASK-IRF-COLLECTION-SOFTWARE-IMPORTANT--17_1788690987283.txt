- loses internet during submission
- retries

- reconnects
- submits the same queued payment

the system must NOT create duplicate payments.

One real payment = one transaction = one receipt.

- Sync successful payment exactly once.
- Update local record to Synced.
- Generate/use the correct unique receipt.
