FINAL A-Z PRODUCTION AUDIT
ISLAMIYA REVOLVING FUND

IMPORTANT:
Perform a complete production-readiness audit of the entire existing application.

This is NOT just a code/build audit.

Audit the system from three perspectives:

1. TECHNICAL LOGIC
2. FINANCIAL / ACCOUNTING LOGIC
3. REAL-WORLD BUSINESS / OPERATIONAL LOGIC

The goal is to determine whether this application is safe to use with REAL MEMBER MONEY.

DO NOT import the real CSV during this audit.
DO NOT create Cycle #02.
DO NOT start real collection.
DO NOT modify genuine historical data.

Before changing anything, inspect the current implementation, database schema, API, frontend, workflows and existing tests.

==================================================
A. DATABASE & DATA INTEGRITY
==================================================

Audit all relevant tables/entities and relationships.

Verify:

- Primary keys
- Foreign keys
- Unique constraints
- Nullability
- Required fields
- Cycle relationships
- Member relationships
- Payment relationships
- Draw relationships
- Transfer relationships
- Settlement relationships
- Audit relationships

Verify there are no orphan records.

Verify financial records cannot accidentally belong to multiple cycles.

Verify completed-cycle records cannot accidentally be overwritten.

Verify database transactions are atomic for sensitive financial operations.

Verify duplicate payment/transfer/draw creation cannot occur because of retries or double submissions.

==================================================
B. PERMANENT MEMBER MASTER
==================================================

Verify that Members are permanent master records.

Each member should support:

- Permanent Member ID
- Name
- Mobile Number
- Active/Inactive status
- Notes
- Created date

IMPORTANT:

Member ID must be separate from mobile number.

Changing name/mobile must NOT change Member ID.

A member leaving a cycle must NOT delete their permanent member record.

Historical records must remain accessible.

Verify duplicate-member prevention/matching logic.

==================================================
C. CYCLE ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Verify that Cycle and Member Master are separate.

Each cycle must independently store:

- Participants
- Shares
- Contribution rules
- Payments
- Collections
- Transfers
- Settlements
- Draws
- Winners
- Reports

Verify:

Cycle #01 data cannot leak into Cycle #02.

Only ONE cycle can be ACTIVE at a time.

A COMPLETED cycle must remain historically frozen.

A new cycle must start with its own participant/share allocation.

A new cycle must NOT automatically inherit old dues/payments.

There must be no hard-coded assumption that a cycle has 108 shares or 108 members.

==================================================
E. SHARE LOGIC
==================================================

Audit the business logic for shares.

Shares are NOT permanent attributes of a member.

Shares are cycle-specific.

A member may have:

Cycle #01 = 6 shares
Cycle #02 = 3 shares
Cycle #03 = 8 shares

Verify previous cycles remain unchanged.

Verify an ACTIVE/NEW cycle allows Owner to edit share quantity.

Verify:

Daily contribution = shares × applicable contribution rate.

Verify share changes do not silently rewrite previous collection/payment history.

==================================================
F. SHARE TRANSFER — HIGH PRIORITY
==================================================

This is one of the most important business rules.

Audit Share Transfer thoroughly.

The system must support:

- Full transfer
- Partial transfer
- Multiple transfers
- Transfer to existing member
- Transfer to new member
- Transfer during an active cycle

There must NOT be a fixed transfer quantity.

Example:

Member has 6 shares.

Allowed:

6 → another member
5 → another member
4 → another member
3 → another member
2 → another member
1 → another member

or multiple transfers such as:

6
↓
2 transferred
remaining 4
↓
1 transferred
remaining 3
↓
3 transferred
remaining 0

Verify the system NEVER allows transfer quantity greater than currently available shares.

Verify negative shares are impossible.

==================================================
G. SHARE TRANSFER FINANCIAL LOGIC
==================================================

Audit whether accumulated contribution correctly follows the transferred share quantity.

Example:

Member A has 6 shares.

2 shares are transferred during the cycle.

The system must determine the accumulated contribution attributable to those 2 shares up to the transfer point.

The original member's historical collection records must NOT be deleted.

The transfer must create a traceable ownership transition.

The receiving member must receive the appropriate transferred share position and accumulated contribution history.

Verify that the system does NOT accidentally transfer the entire member's accumulated contribution when only part of the shares are transferred.

Verify multiple transfers calculate correctly after previous transfers.
